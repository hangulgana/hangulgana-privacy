Privacy Policy — HangulKana Keyboard

Effective date: October 19, 2026 (first effective September 1, 2026)

"HangulKana Keyboard" (the "App") is a keyboard app for typing Japanese with Hangul letters, available for Android and iPhone (iOS). The App does not collect any personal information.

1. What you type

Everything you type is processed only on your device and is never sent off the device. No one, including the developer, can see what you type.

  • The App does not send what you type to any server.
  • The App does not store or record what you type or the candidates you choose, except katakana words you confirm with the ア key, which are stored only on your device. They are never sent off the device and can be deleted with "Clear katakana learning" in Settings.
  • The conversion dictionary is installed with the App, and conversion happens on your device.

2. What is stored on your device

  • Android: only the options you choose in Settings (keyboards to use, keyboard style, kanji candidate display, candidate rows and text size, vibration strength, long-press time).
  • iPhone: only the options you choose in the app (vibration on/off, long-press time, candidate text size), stored on the device in a storage area shared by the app and the keyboard.
  • Both: only katakana words you confirm with the ア key (reading and spelling) are stored on the device, so that the same sound shows that word first next time. They are never sent off the device and can be deleted with "Clear katakana learning" in Settings.
  • These values cannot identify you, are never sent off the device, and are removed when you delete the App.

3. Diagnostic log (off by default)

The App has a diagnostic log used by the developer to investigate conversion errors. It is off by default. While it is on, the typed sounds and candidate lists may be written temporarily to the device's system log (Android system log / iOS unified log). This log is never sent off the device and is not saved as a file.

4. What we do not collect

We collect no personal information of any kind, including name, phone number, email address, location, contacts, photos, files, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage history, or analytics.

5. Internet

The App does not connect to the internet.

  • The Android version does not request the internet permission.
  • The iPhone version contains no networking code. "Allow Full Access" is optional: even with Full Access on, HangulKana never uses the internet and never sends what you type (only katakana words you confirm with the ア key are stored in the keyboard's own folder). Full Access is used only for haptic feedback on key presses. All input features work without it.

6. Advertising

The App has no ads and does not collect or share information for advertising.

7. When you contact us

If you email the developer, your email address and message are used only to reply and to improve the App. They are not shared with third parties and are not kept after the inquiry is handled. Contact happens only when you send it yourself; the App never sends anything automatically.

8. Children

Because the App collects no personal information, it collects none from children.

9. Permissions and device features

  • Android: the system permission needed to work as an input method (IME); vibration (VIBRATE — key feedback, can be turned off in Settings); a new-version check (when you open Settings, the App asks the Google Play app on your device whether a newer version exists and shows a one-line notice at the top of Settings; nothing but the app name and version number is passed). No other permissions (internet, storage, contacts, location, etc.) are requested.
  • iPhone: works as an iOS keyboard extension. "Allow Full Access" is optional and used only for haptic feedback on key presses (without it, everything works with no vibration). Haptics do not play when iPhone "Settings › Sounds & Haptics › System Haptics" is off. Key click sounds follow "Keyboard Feedback › Sound". New-version notices are handled by the App Store.
